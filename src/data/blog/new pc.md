## Moving over

After the 30 min drive back home, I didn't reinstall anything. I just moved my old drive into the new PC & booted it. Linux mostly just went "oh new hardware ok" which was nice.

Removing the NVIDIA drivers also took out some packages I needed, so my login screen, video player & games all broke until I put them back. Spotify also started crashing and my bluetooth would hang on login. All fixed now tho

## Goodbye NVIDIA problems

The best part is that a bunch of game problems I was fighting just went away:

- THE FINALS was a black screen for me on Linux. I had to hide my GPU from the game to get it to show anything.
- Deadlock would freeze mid match because 8GB of VRAM wasn't enough with everything else I had open. I was closing my browser every time I launched a game lmao.
- Marvel Rivals needed its own weird fix too

I deleted all of those fixes. Everything just works now by default, thanks AMD!

## The monitor

Going from 2k -> 4k was an actual crazy jump for me. When i play games in 4k the metal actually looks real now!?

I love the quality difference, and being able to play games on ultra. Everything looks the way it was intended to, and its super nice.

I am excited to see how this thing takes GTA6 whenever that comes to PC (this game coming out was part of the reason I wanted to upgrade in the first place too)
