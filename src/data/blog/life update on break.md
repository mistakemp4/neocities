## Cyberpunk 2077: Everyone Is Awful

I finally finished _Cyberpunk 2077_ last night, and wow what a game. I went with the Panam ending, which felt perfect for my Nomad-background V. It had that bittersweet sense of freedom that fits Cyberpunk’s world without being _completely_ soul-crushing.

Afterward, my girlfriend info-dumped the other endings to me (Cyberpunk is her current special interest), and honestly? I think I picked the best possible option anyway. I briefly considered letting Johnny take over my body to go fight alongside the AI girl, but ultimately decided against it because, frankly, fuck Johnny. He is the worst.

One of my favorite things about Cyberpunk is that almost everyone is a terrible person in some way. It’s messy, morally gross, and painfully human and that’s what makes it so good. No one really wins, and V especially never gets a clean or happy ending. According to my gf, the DLC endings are even worse than the base game ones, which feels almost impressive in how cruel they are. Poor V. Cyberpunk truly never ends well.

## Back to Tartarus

After finishing Cyberpunk, I jumped into _Persona 3 Portable_, which Chloe very kindly gifted me on Steam for Christmas (thank you Chloe). This time I’m playing as Kotone. I already played Reload with Makoto as the protagonist, so getting to experience P3 from Kotone’s perspective feels fresh.

I’m only about four hours in so far, but I’m having a lot of fun. Going back to an older Persona game definitely takes some adjustment bc fusion feels more restrictive, and I miss some of the quality-of-life features from the newer entries, but it’s still Persona, and I love Persona~

What I’m most excited about is seeing how the story and social links differ with Kotone, and, most importantly, finally getting to romance a man in a Persona game. Extremely important gameplay feature. I am looking forward to this deeply.
