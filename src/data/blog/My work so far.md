## OK but how actually IS it?

HARD… and humbling….

My boss and I are still working on our communication. she has a very hands-off management style and I have a very "wait what did you mean by that" listening style.

The actual work is thousands of steps, each with its own little chemistry lesson behind it, and you have to understand how all of it works before you can even start doing stats on it. On top of that im running on stats 1 and the business stats classes, so every meeting is about 40% acronyms I nod along to and then google under the desk. I barely remember wtf a sigma is and I am supposedly the stats person.

Case in point: I once put the wrong chart in a ppt bc it "looked worse" (which, to me, felt like the point??) and got told it was wrong, and I still couldnt tell you whyyy. Very much learning on the job. Lots of learning. So much learning.
